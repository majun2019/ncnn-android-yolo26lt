Subject: Submission of manuscript entitled "An Android-NCNN Deployment and Consistency Diagnosis Framework for Multi-Task YOLO-Based PPE Monitoring on Resource-Constrained Devices"

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "An Android-NCNN Deployment and Consistency Diagnosis Framework for Multi-Task YOLO-Based PPE Monitoring on Resource-Constrained Devices" for consideration as an Article in Applied Sciences.

This work is positioned as an applied mobile computer-vision and edge-deployment study for construction-site PPE monitoring, rather than as a new detector-architecture paper. The manuscript presents:

1. An applied Java-JNI-C++-NCNN deployment framework that supports detection, segmentation, pose estimation, classification, and OBB within one Android application under coexisting E2E, One-to-Many, and Legacy output paths.
2. A five-stage deployment consistency diagnosis workflow (anomaly logging, intermediate-output inspection, cross-backend comparison, structure tracing, regression validation) for localization and repair of deployment deviations.
3. Reproducible quantitative repair evidence from the SafeHat main case, including empty-scene median score reduction (0.84-0.96 to < 0.0001), restoration of raw probability distribution after removing redundant activation ($p_{50}\approx 0.34$, $p_{95}\approx 0.74$), calibrated UI confidence stability (0.35-0.73), and runtime post-processing sensitivity results.
4. Real-device CPU-path validation on two Android phones, providing an implementation-level feasibility baseline for resource-constrained deployment.

We believe the manuscript fits Applied Sciences because it focuses on practical engineering validation, reproducibility, and real-device evidence for an important safety-monitoring application domain.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to Applied Sciences.
